# Next expansion proposal — not implemented

## New vehicles before more waves
- Helicopter: hovering guard, dangerous armored rotor on top, exposed underside. Rising underside contact deals 2 damage; top contact hurts the player and leaves the rotor intact. Telegraph rotor danger and highlight the belly. Start with one helicopter and a safe tutorial arena. Bullets only damage the exposed belly so the rule remains consistent.
- Interceptor jet: fast straight passes with a long turn-around window; fragile rear engine. Distinct from the current bonus jet, which retains its reward rules.
- Cargo plane: slow armored nose, vulnerable rear; drops supply crates when defeated.
- Autogyro: slow turns and drifting dives, exposed engine; teaches timing between the biplane and helicopter encounters.

## Sector structure
Expand each sector from three to five stages: introduction, specialty-vehicle mission, objective remix, boss, escape. Preserve existing checkpoint and grade saves with stable stage IDs and a migration; do not reinterpret old stage numbers. Add content progressively rather than padding waves.

## Score objectives
Use score attack in selected missions, e.g. 3,000 points in 90 seconds, with capped reinforcements and a visible score target. Teach rescues, chains and ground targets. Do not gate every mission by score: defense, rescue and bosses should retain their own objectives. Tune thresholds from playtests; the values above are starting hypotheses.

## Higher ceiling
Prototype a taller world with vertical camera follow and a horizon/altitude indicator. Merely moving the current ceiling upward gives little space and makes art smaller. Preserve the stall/rebound rule so ceiling camping cannot return. Validate enemy tracking, mines, bomb arcs, scenery and mobile readability before rollout.

## Suggested order
1. Helicopter tutorial and underside combat.
2. Two more vehicle types, each with a clearly taught weakness.
3. Five-stage sector structure with checkpoint migration.
4. Score-attack missions and medals.
5. Taller-airspace prototype, then balance playtests.
