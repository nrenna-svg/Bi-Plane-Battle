# Kestrel Bay v9.18
Four features (+0.08): navigable coastal landmarks and route HUD; optional radar with lighter patrol/ace branch and two encounter orders; harbor assault with three depots, three patrols and persistent crane/fire damage; five permanent replay mastery stars.
The eight-minute mission window accommodates the intended 5–7 minute rookie sortie; skilled pilots may finish sooner. No forced waiting to pad the duration. Patrols are announced, one or two enemies per harbor wave, and must be cleared before the next wave.
Carrier at x600, Needle Point x1100, convoy x1450–2120, harbor x2240–2760. Existing 3000m wrapping flight world is retained. This is a connected coastal route, not a new endless world.
Radar destruction slows fire and convoy reinforcement cadence, halves harbor patrols, and removes Scarlet Finch’s escort.
Mastery: carrier return, radar disabled, all three lives, two rescues, under six minutes. First win pays 200; each new star pays 10 once; failure awards neither. Existing careers and island victories survive migration. Bay mastery is tracked separately from legacy campaign stars.
Validation: branch/state tests, rendering in normal and reduced motion, save migration, reward anti-farming, responsive result card. Completion-time balance still needs human playtesting with the stock Scout.
