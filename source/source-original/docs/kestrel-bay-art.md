# Kestrel Bay panorama

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Runtime asset: `public/scenery/kestrel-bay-v1.png`. Animated vessels, surf, smoke and wrecks are rendered separately in Canvas.

## Final generation prompt

Create a high-detail photorealistic cinematic matte painting used as a seamless side-scrolling 2D aerial combat videogame background. Wide landscape 16:9. Kestrel Bay, a rugged northern Atlantic coastal inlet during a WWII-era storm at late golden hour. Upper 65 percent mostly open dramatic layered slate clouds with a warm break in the clouds, readable negative space for small aircraft. Horizon at 70 percent height. Lower 30 percent: realistic choppy blue-grey ocean with white foam and shimmering light, rugged distant sea cliffs at left and right, a small distant harbor along the horizon, atmospheric mist. Side-on low-altitude aerial view, grounded perspective and natural rocky textures. No aircraft, no large foreground buildings, no roads, no ships, no explosions, no text, no UI, no borders. Detailed realistic environment, not low poly, not cartoon. The very bottom edge is dark sea; game foreground will be layered on top. Keep edges approximately matching in sea and cloud tone for mirrored scrolling.
