## Prompt set
Landscape prompt shared constraints: cinematic photorealistic videogame matte painting, wide 2:1 view, distant low horizon, abundant open atmospheric sky for flight, real materials and natural proportions; no aircraft, people, HUD, text, cartoon forms or low-poly geometry.
- Harbor: 1930s North Atlantic docks, weathered warehouses, lattice cranes, ship masts, coastal hills, sea mist and reflective choppy water under storm clouds.
- Forest: realistic spruce and broadleaf forest valley, jade river, waterfall on weathered cliffs, layered wooded hills in golden morning mist.
- City: 1930s European industrial city at dusk, brick factories, sawtooth and slate roofs, stone railway viaduct, art deco towers, chimney smoke and glowing windows.
- Desert: American southwest sandstone mesas, stratified eroded cliffs, dunes and scrub in copper sunset light beneath peach clouds.
- Mountain: snow-covered alpine ridges, exposed rock, dark conifers and a stone ridge fortress with icy blue atmospheric haze.
- Warehouse: transparent straight-on 1930s factory sprite, flat collision-compatible parapet, weathered masonry, recessed arched amber windows, cornice, gutters and iron loading doors; no ground, sky or text.
- Cloud: transparent wide isolated photorealistic cumulus cloud, feathered vapor edges, silver highlights and blue-gray shaded underside; no cartoon outlines or landscape.

Backgrounds use cached images with smooth mirrored joins and continuous camera tracking across world wrapping. Existing procedural scenery remains available if an image cannot load. Existing collision boundaries and weather are retained.
